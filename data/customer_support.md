# Demo Customer Support Policy

This fictional policy is for a software demonstration.
It does not describe a real company's customer support service.

## Working Hours

Customer support operates Sunday through Thursday,
from 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM, Cairo local time.
Support is closed on public holidays.

## Complaints

Customers submitting a complaint should provide their customer
reference number, a description of the issue, and relevant documents.
Customers should never include passwords or account PINs.

## Complaint Acknowledgment

The support team acknowledges complaints within two business days.
Acknowledgment confirms receipt and does not mean the issue is resolved.

## Complaint Resolution

The target resolution time is ten business days.
Complex cases may take longer, and customers should receive an update.

## Updating Contact Details

Customers must complete identity verification before changing
their registered phone number or email address.
