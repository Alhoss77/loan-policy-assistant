# Demo Motor Insurance Policy

This fictional policy is for a software demonstration.
It does not describe products offered by a real insurer.

## Coverage

The policy covers accidental damage to the insured vehicle,
vehicle theft, and damage caused by fire, subject to policy conditions.

## Exclusions

The policy excludes normal wear and tear, mechanical breakdown,
and damage occurring while the vehicle is used for unauthorized racing.

## Claim Documents

A claim requires:
- The policy number.
- A copy of the driver's license.
- Photographs of the damage, when available.
- A police report for theft claims.

## Claim Notification

Customers must notify the insurer within 48 hours
of discovering the incident.

## Deductible

The deductible is the amount the customer contributes toward
a covered claim. Its value is specified in the individual policy schedule.
This document does not provide a fixed deductible amount.
