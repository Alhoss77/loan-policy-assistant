# Demo Personal Loan Policy

This fictional policy is for a software demonstration.
It does not describe products offered by a real financial institution.

## Eligibility

Applicants must be at least 21 years old.
Applicants must have a verifiable source of income.
Meeting these requirements does not guarantee loan approval.

## Required Documents

Applicants must provide:
- A valid national ID.
- Proof of income covering the last three months.
- Proof of residential address.

## Repayment Period

Available repayment periods range from 12 to 60 months.
Repayments are made monthly.

## Interest Rates and Fees

This policy does not specify interest rates, processing fees,
or late payment charges. These must be confirmed in the loan offer.

## Early Repayment

Customers may request early repayment.
Any applicable early repayment charge must be confirmed
in the customer's loan agreement.
